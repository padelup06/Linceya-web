Privacy
Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Linceya ("we", "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it and your rights.

Data controller

Linceya
Contact: contact@linceya.com

Data collected

  • Account: email address, username, profile picture
  • Sports profile: playing level, city/region, court position
  • Matches: scores, results, match history
  • Videos: match videos (image and sound) uploaded for AI analysis and the match replay (Elite analysis); if you agree, a copy of the in-play parts, without sound, to train our models
  • Your choice about training our models: your answer, its date, the language and version of the text you saw, where you answered (an app screen or a message), the date you closed the question without answering, and, if your account is deleted while your answer was still "Yes," the date it was deleted
  • AI analyses: scores, metrics, personalized coaching
  • Subscription: status of your Elite subscription (managed by RevenueCat)
  • Free month and first payment: the date of your first Elite payment (on linceya.com, the App Store or Google Play), with or without a free month; if your Elite subscription starts with a free month, its dates (start, end and, if the App Store or Google Play cannot collect your first payment, the end of the period it gives you to settle it) and, for a subscription transferred from another Linceya account, the number of analyses of that free month already used. After a free month, the date of the first payment opens your 4 analyses per month; this information is also used so that the linceya.com free month is not given to an account that has already had a free month or paid for Elite. It is part of your profile, like the status of your subscription: it is copied, with your profile, to the phones of your friends in the app (people you added and people who added you), to the phone of the coach linked to your account, including while an invitation is pending, and, if you are a coach, to the phones of the players linked to you as students, including while an invitation is pending or after it was declined. Like the rest of your profile, it can also be read by the app of any other signed-in user, for example when they search for your username or open a league you play in.
  • Technical identifiers: push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), install identifier, Android advertising ID
  • Geolocation: approximate city/region to suggest nearby clubs (only with your explicit consent)
  • Diagnostic data: anonymized error reports via Sentry (stack trace, OS version, technical context — no personal content)
  • Usage data: in-app navigation events (screens viewed, subscription offer displayed, plan selected, purchase completed or abandoned) and subscription events (sign-up, start and end of the free month, renewal, cancellation, refund); the free month events include its end date, and those of a subscription bought on linceya.com without a Linceya account are linked, since November 26, 2026, to a fingerprint of your email address (a code derived from the address): the address itself is no longer sent to our product analytics tool (PostHog)

Use of data

  • Calculate and display your certified ELO
  • Generate AI analyses of your matches: the Elite analysis, on machines operated by Linceya (the classic analysis via Gemini, from Google, is no longer offered; past classic analyses remain available)
  • Create the replay of your Elite matches and, when sharing is available, the videos you can choose to share
  • Personalize your training plan
  • Display regional and national rankings
  • Manage your Elite subscription
  • Send you push notifications about your matches and analyses
  • Detect and fix bugs (Sentry diagnostics)
  • Train our models with your match videos, only if you agreed to it
  • Improve the app

Once received by Cloudflare, emails sent to contact@linceya.com go to a Gmail inbox. In the European Economic Area and Switzerland, Gmail is provided by Google Ireland Limited (Ireland). On the linceya.com website only — these tools are not present in the mobile app:

Match videos

Uploaded videos are used for AI analysis and, for the Elite analysis, for the match replay and the short videos to share (when sharing is available) described below. They're used to train our models only if you agreed to it (see "Improving our models" below). No video is sent to Google: the classic analysis, which sent them to the Google Gemini API, is no longer offered. The Elite analysis is computed on machines operated by Linceya: the video is transferred there from our storage space for the analysis. Google Gemini only receives numerical measurements from your match (number and types of shots, distance, speeds, time at the net and at the back of the court, duration analyzed), without any images, to write your coaching texts and your training plan. With each Elite analysis, Linceya also keeps a small image (192 pixels wide) of each player in the match, four at most, cropped from the video at the moment they are most visible: it may show their face and outfit. In the app, these images let you recognize yourself and indicate who the other players are. They are stored with the analysis in our database (Supabase, servers in Switzerland), kept as long as the match exists in your account, and erased from this database when the match or your account is deleted. If you have linked a coach to your account, they can also see your analyses, including these images. We do not share your videos with other users.

The sound in your videos. Videos filmed with the app also contain the sound recorded by the phone's microphone, which may pick up voices and conversations around the court; a video chosen from your gallery keeps whatever sound it contains. This sound is uploaded and kept with the video and its working copies, never separately, and is deleted with them. A match filmed with the app is also copied, sound included, to your phone's gallery. On Android, from version 10 onwards, the copy is made without asking for your permission. On iPhone, on iPad and on older versions of Android, it is only made with your permission, which the device asks you for and which you can change in its settings. This copy becomes a video like any other in your gallery. Only you manage it, it may be backed up online along with your gallery if you have chosen to (for example with iCloud or Google Photos), and deleting the match or your account does not erase it. The Elite analysis does not use the sound, and the videos made from your match for the replay (rally clips shown in the app, videos to share) do not include it. A video sent to have your level assessed is watched and listened to by a Linceya coach, who validates that level.

Match replay (Elite analysis). After an Elite analysis, Linceya may keep an animated summary of a few rallies from the match: your movement on court (path, distance, speed) and where the other players were, stored as plain positions with no image and no identity. This summary is kept as long as the match exists in your account, and erased after the match or your account is deleted. When sharing is available, Linceya also creates short vertical videos of these rallies from your match video, along with preview images. They are stored in a private space reserved for your account: only you can download them, from the app. They are deleted after 30 days, or sooner if the match is deleted. These videos may show the other players on the court: ask for their consent before posting them. Linceya never posts these videos for you: nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

Rally clips (Elite analysis). When this feature is available, the replay also shows, in the app, the video of each of its rallies (three at most), cut from your match video, without the sound. Unlike the animated summary, these clips are real footage of the match. They show the court, and therefore the other players and the people around the court too, who may be recognizable in them. Like the videos to share, they are stored in the private space reserved for your account. Only you can watch them, from the app. They are deleted from this space after 30 days, or sooner if the match or your account is deleted. To play them, including offline, the app downloads a copy to your phone, into its own storage space and not into your gallery. When you open a replay, the app automatically deletes the copies that have not been used for 7 days. Deleting the match or your account does not erase them right away. They are erased by this clean-up, or along with the app when you delete it. Linceya never posts these clips, and the app does not let you share them. Only the videos to share described above, which show these same rallies, can be shared, and only if you choose to.

Improving our models (only with your consent)

  1. When this feature is available, the app asks you once: the first time you open an Elite analysis with a version of the app that offers this feature, and again if this text changes. The question is: "Do you agree that Linceya may keep your next match videos, without sound, to train its models?" The two answers, "Yes" and "No thanks," are shown the same way, and neither is selected in advance. Your analysis is the same either way. We record your answer with its date, its language, and the version of the text you saw. You can see it and change it at any time in Profile > Help Linceya get better. By answering "Yes," you confirm that you're 16 or older.
  2. If you answered "Yes," for each video uploaded after your answer and processed by the Elite analysis, Linceya keeps: the in-play parts of the video (downtime is removed), without sound; the ball and player positions, including body points, computed during the analysis; the shots detected; and whether the analysis succeeded. We never keep the sound, the location information stored in the video file, or the descriptions of the players' clothing. A video uploaded before your "Yes," or while your answer was "No," is never kept, even if you say "Yes" later. A video uploaded from a version of the app that doesn't offer this feature yet is kept the same way if your answer was already "Yes" before it reached our servers; that version doesn't show the upload screen notice or "Not this video." A video is kept only if you indicated in the app which player you are. On the upload screen, "Not this video" turns down one video: it isn't kept, and your answer stays "Yes" for the next ones. Tap it for a match you don't play in, or with a player under 16. If you often film matches you don't play in, answer "No" instead. A video is never kept for a match created from a Linceya public match.
  3. Purpose: to teach our models to better spot the ball, the players, and the shots. These videos are never used to recognize a person, to find out who they are, or to identify them from one match to another. Nor are they used to learn to follow or tell players apart by their appearance or clothing. They aren't used for the models built into the app on your phone.
  4. The copy is stored in a private Linceya storage space at Supabase, on servers in Switzerland (Zurich). No user can access it. To annotate the videos and train the models, the person who runs Linceya downloads copies to a Linceya computer. That person watches and annotates them, marking by hand or with their own tools where the ball and the players are and which shots are played. For this work, they may get help from Claude, Anthropic's AI. Claude can see images taken from these videos, and the data computed with them, to help label them and check the datasets. Anthropic then acts as Linceya's processor (United States, see section 04), under its commercial terms, which include its data processing agreement. It doesn't use these images or this data to train its models. It deletes them from its servers no later than 30 days after they're sent. One exception: if its automated safety filters flag something we sent, Anthropic may keep it for up to 2 years, along with Claude's answer, and the result of that check for up to 7 years, to enforce its usage policy. These videos are never posted, sold, rented, used for advertising, or shown to other users. They're never sold or passed on to another company for its own use (Supabase stores them and Anthropic helps analyze them, only on our behalf, apart from the safety exception above), or to any other outside AI service, Google Gemini included. Before anyone else takes part (an annotator, a computing provider), we'll update this policy.
  5. For the 7 days after the copy is made, it isn't used, so you can change your mind. It goes into a dataset when images or positions taken from it are used to train or evaluate a model. If it doesn't go into any dataset, it's deleted 6 months after the match. Otherwise, it's deleted no later than 2 years after the match. The copies, images, and annotations taken from it are deleted within the following 30 days, except for Anthropic's exception in point 4.
  6. You can answer "No" at any time, in one tap, in Profile > Help Linceya get better. You can also tell us in writing (contact form or contact@linceya.com). From then on, no video is kept, and the ones already kept are deleted within 30 days, along with everything taken from them, except for Anthropic's exception in point 4. Models that already learned from them are retrained without them within 12 months; until then, they aren't used for any new training. Your "No" doesn't affect what was done before. If the match or your account is deleted, these videos and everything taken from them are also deleted within 30 days, with the same exception, and our models are retrained without them within 12 months.
  7. A match video also shows the other players, and sometimes people around the court. For them, Linceya relies on its legitimate interest in improving its models (GDPR, Article 6(1)(f)), with these safeguards: no sound, in-play parts only, never posted, access limited to the person who runs Linceya and to Claude, Anthropic's AI, which helps as our processor (except for Anthropic's exception in point 4), limited retention, and no recognition of anyone.
  8. Are you in a video and don't want it kept? These videos are sent to us, through the app, by a player in your match. You can object to them being kept, with or without a Linceya account, without giving a reason. A parent can make the request for their child. Use the contact form ("I'm in a video" subject) or write to contact@linceya.com. Give the date and time of the match, the club, city, and court, and what you were wearing. We'll look for the video. If we find it, we'll delete it within 30 days, along with everything taken from it (except for Anthropic's exception in point 4), and our models will be retrained without it within 12 months. If we can't find it, we'll tell you. Your objection applies to the video we find. If you often play with the same person, tell us, and we'll stop keeping any video sent from their account for 2 years. You can also ask us whether a video you appear in is kept, ask for it to be deleted, and file a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr) or the data protection authority in your country. For this search, we don't create any face files and don't use facial recognition.

Legal basis for processing

In accordance with GDPR (Article 6), we process your data on the following bases:

  • Performance of contract — to provide the Linceya service (AI analysis, ELO, ranking, subscription management)
  • Consent — for geolocation, push notifications and, if you agree, keeping your match videos to train our models (you can withdraw your consent at any time in the app)
  • Legitimate interest — to improve the app (videos excluded) and prevent fraud, and, for the other people visible in a video kept with the uploader's consent, to improve our models (see "Improving our models")
  • Legal obligation — to keep billing data, and proof of your choice about training our models (GDPR, Article 7)

Data retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. If your account is deleted, all your personal data is removed within 8 days, with four exceptions: the data of a subscription bought on linceya.com (contract, withdrawal and cancellation requests, copies of the emails about them), kept for our legal obligations and as proof of the contract (see below); videos kept to train our models, and everything taken from them, deleted within 30 days (except for Anthropic's exception, see below); the history of your choices about this use (answer, date, language, text version, where you answered, the date you closed the question without answering, and, if your account is deleted while your answer was still "Yes," the date it was deleted), kept for 5 years after your last choice as proof of your consent, with your account's technical ID but without your name or email address; and the messages you sent us by email or through the website's forms, kept for 12 months after our last reply or, if we don't reply, after we receive them, except requests and emails about a subscription bought on linceya.com, kept for 5 years (see below). As long as your account exists and your answer is "Yes," this history is never deleted. If your account is deleted, this history is kept for 5 years after the deletion if your answer was still "Yes," and removed if you never answered "Yes."

If you subscribed on linceya.com without a Linceya account, the activation code you received by email is also part of the subscription data kept after your account is deleted, with your email address: it is deleted 3 years after it was used, or 3 years after its expiration date if it was never used; a code that was never used may be deleted earlier (see below).

Messages sent to contact@linceya.com or through the website's forms are kept for 12 months after our last reply or, if we don't reply, after we receive them, then deleted, whether or not you have an account. Exception: requests and emails about a subscription bought on linceya.com (withdrawal, cancellation, and the copies of the emails about them) are kept for 5 years, see below.

Subscription bought on linceya.com. This data is kept for our legal obligations and as proof of the contract, whether or not your account is deleted:

  • the contract (plan, price, dates, version of the terms of sale accepted, request for immediate access, Stripe identifiers) is kept for 10 years after it ends, with the monthly plan as with the annual plan. It does not contain your email address, only its fingerprint: a code derived from the address, which lets us find your contract without keeping the address in plain text;
  • to give the free month only once per person, the contract also keeps a code derived from a shortened form of your email address (without what follows a "+", for example). If your subscription starts with a free month, it also keeps the dates of that free month and a code derived from your card, never its number. These codes and dates are deleted with the contract, 10 years after it ends;
  • if you subscribe without a Linceya account, the activation code you receive by email is kept with your email address. If your subscription starts with a free month, the dates of that free month are kept with this code until they are copied to your Linceya account, then deleted from the code. If they are not copied, they are deleted with the code, or earlier. Each activation code is deleted 3 years after it is used, or 3 years after its expiration date if it was never used; a code that was never used may be deleted earlier;
  • withdrawal and cancellation requests made online are kept for 5 years after we receive them, without your name, your address or your message (the address is kept only long enough to send the acknowledgment of receipt, 48 hours at most); a request sent by email is also kept for 5 years;
  • the emails about your subscription are sent through Resend, our email delivery provider. The acknowledgments of receipt, the refund emails, the confirmations of cancellation or of the end of the subscription and the cancellation of a withdrawal also arrive, as a copy, in our contact@linceya.com mailbox: these copies, which include your name, your email address and the content of your requests, are kept for 5 years;
  • invoices and payment data (the name, email address and billing address given at payment, amounts, payment method) are held by Stripe, our payment provider: we keep them for 10 years, as the law requires for accounting records. Deleting your Linceya account does not delete them.

First payment and free month dates. The date of your first payment and those of your free month (see "Free month and first payment" above) are kept with your Linceya account for as long as it exists, and deleted with it. Exception: the dates of a free month taken on linceya.com and canceled because its payment method was not a card we could verify are removed from your account after that cancellation; the contract keeps them (see above).

Elite analysis working files. On the Linceya machines that compute the Elite analysis, the working files, in particular the player positions computed during the analysis, are kept so that you can, if needed, indicate which player you are ("Which one is you?") or who's who between two players wearing identical jerseys. These files, along with the working copies of your video, are deleted no later than 30 days after they were last used (for example for an analysis or a correction), even if the analysis was never completed, and sooner if the match is deleted.

Copies kept to train our models. A copy is kept only if you agreed to help train our models: 6 months after the match if it doesn't go into any dataset, and no more than 2 years otherwise (see "Improving our models"). Images and data sent to Claude, Anthropic's AI, for analysis are deleted by Anthropic no later than 30 days after they're sent. Exception: anything we sent that its automated safety filters flag may be kept for up to 2 years, along with Claude's answer, and the result of that check for up to 7 years, to enforce its usage policy.

Video analyses started. To count the video analyses of your Elite subscription, we keep a list of the analyses you start: for each one, your account, the match and the time it was started. An analysis stays on this list even if the match is deleted later, unless it was canceled, or its match deleted, before its processing began: it is then removed from the list right away. Reserving a machine for this analysis is part of its processing. Each analysis is deleted from this list 13 months after it was started, or sooner if your account is deleted.

Security

Children

Linceya is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data on minors under 13.

You must be 16 or older to help train our models. For a match with a player under 16, tap "Not this video" on the upload screen: that video is never kept. You can also answer "No" in Profile > Help Linceya get better before uploading the video: a video uploaded while your answer is "No" is never kept, even if you say "Yes" again later, and your videos already kept are deleted. If your app doesn't have this link: write to us (section 08), and we'll delete the video. If we notice, when watching a video, that a player is clearly under 16, or that the person who uploaded it doesn't play, we delete it.
