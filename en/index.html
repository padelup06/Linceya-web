FREQUENTLYASKED

Upload your match video from the app. Our AI analyzes every frame to detect your shots, movements and tactical patterns. The analysis generates a full report with your ELO score and your improvement areas.
Ideally use a fixed elevated camera that captures the whole court. A smartphone on a tripod works great. The AI automatically identifies the 4 players and asks you to select your profile before the analysis.
Linceya offers a free version with limited analysis. The Premium subscription (€9.99/month or €99.99/year) gives access to 10 AI video analyses per month, the full personalized training plan and certified ELO ranking.
Elite includes everything in Premium and adds advanced AI analysis powered by computer vision: court coverage heatmap, automatic shot and movement detection, distance covered, ball speed, positioning (net/mid/back) and shot count by type. It's the plan for competitors who want in-depth match stats. It launches soon — the Elite card will switch from 'Coming soon' to an activable subscription.
Your videos are processed securely and never shared with third parties. You can delete your data at any time from the app or via our account deletion page.
Linceya is available on Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store). Download the app for free and start analyzing your matches today.
The key to progressing at padel is precisely identifying your weaknesses. Linceya analyzes your matches with AI and generates a training plan targeted at your real improvement areas: technique, net play, reaction time, positioning. On average, our users gain 2 levels in 3 weeks.
The ELO system is a numerical ranking that evaluates your real level based on match performance. Unlike traditional rankings, the Linceya ELO is automatically calculated by our AI after every video analysis. It updates every match and lets you compare yourself to players in your region and across the country.
Absolutely. Linceya is used both for training and competition. Film your match at the club — the AI automatically recognizes the 4 players on court. You can also join public matches and find partners at your level in partner clubs near you.
Each analysis delivers 12 key points: your overall score, 4 rated metrics (stability, net play, tactics, consistency), your 2 strengths from the match, 3 improvement areas, your critical error to fix first and a personalized coaching plan. All generated by AI from the video — no manual input.
Linceya is an ideal complement to a padel coach. The AI analyzes your padel match and catches details even a coach might miss (like net approach timing or repetitive patterns). Many padel coaches use Linceya to enrich their analyses and offer more targeted drills to their students.
