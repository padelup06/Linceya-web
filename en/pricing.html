ABOUT THESUBSCRIPTION

Free gives you the ELO ranking, global and regional rankings and public matches (after a free evaluation video approved by our coach), plus leagues with friends, with no card required and no video analysis. Elite adds 4 AI video analyses per month, the AI personalized training plan, certified ELO, full progress history and coach invitations.
Elite is Linceya's video analysis: 4 analyses per month, powered by computer vision. Our AI follows your match frame by frame: court coverage heatmap, automatic detection of shots (estimated count) and movements, distance covered, movement speeds (average and max), time at the net and at the back. You also get, when the match allows a review, your strengths, improvement areas and critical error, plus a personalized training plan. It's available at €24.99/month or €199/year here, or €29.99/month or €249.99/year from the app, with 4 analyses a month either way (2 in total during the free month).
Yes: 1 month free for a first subscription, monthly or yearly, with 2 video analyses. Card required at sign-up. When the free month ends, the subscription becomes paid automatically: €24.99/month or €199/year here, €29.99/month or €249.99/year in the app. You can cancel before the free month ends, here from “Cancel your contract”, or in the app from the App Store (at least 24 hours before) or Google Play: nothing is charged. The free version, for its part, has no time limit and needs no card.
Yes. The monthly subscription is no-commitment: you can cancel anytime, via “Cancel your contract” or “Manage my subscription” on this page if you subscribed on the website, or from your Google Play or App Store account settings if you subscribed in the app. The yearly plan on the stores is paid 12 months upfront: you can stop its renewal anytime. The site's annual subscription (€199) is a 12-month commitment: you can refuse its renewal until the day before it renews, with “Cancel your contract”; we remind you by email beforehand. With the free month, this commitment starts at your first payment, when the free month ends. After cancelling, you keep Elite until the end of your paid period, or of your free month.
No, all your analyses, scores and ELO data are kept. Going Elite simply unlocks advanced features on your existing account.
On the website, payment is secured by Stripe: credit or debit card (Apple Pay, Google Pay and Link included), Klarna or Amazon Pay; with the free month, credit or debit card only (Apple Pay and Google Pay included). In the app, the subscription goes through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). Credit card, PayPal or store credit are accepted depending on your setup.
On iOS and Android, Apple and Google take up to 30% commission on every subscription purchased inside the app — that extra cost is reflected in the in-store price. By subscribing directly here, payment goes through Stripe and you get our base price (€24.99/month or €199/year). The features are exactly the same, whichever option you choose.
